# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

French-speaking fans of the streaming site Movix, who watch films, series and anime there and want the site to look the way they like without touching code: they pick a theme, nudge a colour or two, and get back to watching. A smaller group of enthusiasts goes further, crafting detailed themes (custom colours, extra CSS) and sharing them as files. Both use Motix in short sessions, usually in the evening with a film or episode paused, from the browser toolbar or straight from the Movix page.

## Product Purpose

Motix restyles Movix from the outside: colours, corners, depth, button size and lettering, applied instantly and kept on the user's device. Success is a fan who finds a theme they love within a minute, sees it on the real site as they tweak it, and never wonders where it was saved or whether they lost their changes.

## Positioning

Ton Movix, à ton image: the only way to make Movix truly your own, in a few seconds.

## Brand Personality

Playful, fan, crafted. The tool speaks like a fellow fan, warm and direct, never like a SaaS onboarding. It has the cinephile warmth of Letterboxd, a nod to anime culture in the spirit of Crunchyroll, and the playful-but-precise feel of Arc Browser, where the tool takes on the colours you give it. Personality comes from the themes themselves, the poster-like previews and small moments of delight, not from decoration.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS (eyebrow kickers, gradients and glows, interchangeable onboarding copy). Loaded neon gamer aesthetics (RGB, glows everywhere, aggressive effects). A cold technical settings panel full of jargon. An amateur fan site (decorative clutter, emojis everywhere, inconsistent components).

## Design Principles

- **Show it on the real thing.** Every change is visible on Movix itself as it happens; a mock-up is only a fallback.
- **One click to a great look, depth on demand.** Picking a theme is enough; colours, shape and CSS unfold only for those who want them.
- **Never lose the user's work.** Where a theme is saved and what is unsaved are always visible, and nothing destructive happens without a way back.
- **The themes are the stars.** The tool stays quiet and lets each theme's colours carry the personality.
- **Speak like a fan, in their language.** French first, English as a fallback, plain words over settings jargon.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 text contrast, 3:1 for controls and focus indicators, full keyboard use, labelled controls with their help text attached, state changes announced to screen readers, and reduced motion honoured everywhere.
